My story

From mathematical rigor to visual clarity.

My background in mathematics trained me to think precisely, question assumptions, and look for the underlying structure in complex systems. Over time, I became increasingly interested in how analysis becomes useful only when people can actually understand it.

That is what drew me toward data visualization and analytical storytelling. I enjoy translating ambiguity into structure, patterns into insight, and dense information into something decision-makers can trust and act on.

How I work

Clear thinking, restrained design, meaningful outcomes.

  • Start with the question, not the chart.
  • Reduce noise until the signal becomes obvious.
  • Use design to support understanding, never decoration.
  • Balance analytical depth with editorial clarity.
  • Build for both insight and trust.
