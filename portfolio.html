Impact Dashboard

Noise Solution Impact Story

An interactive impact dashboard designed to keep the human story at the center of the data. The project combines participant journeys, reflective measures, and qualitative insights to explore how confidence, connection, and wellbeing evolve through music mentoring.

Built around wellbeing, engagement, and Self Determination Theory (SDT) metrics, the dashboard blends quantitative indicators with participant voices to create a more accessible and emotionally grounded view of impact.

Interactive Visualization

Gapminder Bubble Plot

A polished interactive bubble plot built from scratch using the Gapminder dataset, exploring global development patterns through GDP, life expectancy, and population data.

The project focuses on reusable chart architecture, including custom axis components, margin and translation systems, and scalable styling patterns. Inspired by Hans Rosling's iconic presentations, the project translates one of the most recognizable datasets in data visualization into a modern web experience.
